Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

1. Introduction

Mukunda Infraventure & Projects Pvt. Ltd. ("Mukunda", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through our website, how we use it, and the choices you have. It is intended to be consistent with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

2. Information We Collect
  • Information you provide: name, phone number, email address, city, project of interest, and any message you send through enquiry or contact forms.
  • Information collected automatically: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and time spent, through cookies and analytics tools.
3. How We Use Your Information
  • To respond to your enquiries and share details about our projects.
  • To arrange site visits and follow up on your interest.
  • To send updates, offers and project announcements (you can opt out at any time).
  • To improve our website, services and marketing.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations.
4. Cookies and Analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies to make the website work, analyse traffic, and measure marketing performance. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work properly.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Our employees, sales partners and channel partners who assist with your enquiry.
  • Service providers such as hosting, analytics, CRM and communication platforms, who process data on our behalf.
  • Government or regulatory authorities when required by law.
6. Third-Party Services

Our website embeds or links to services such as Google Maps, Facebook, YouTube, LinkedIn and Instagram. These services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to read them.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We keep your information only for as long as needed for the purposes described above, or as required by law.

9. Your Rights

You may request to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct or update inaccurate information.
  • Withdraw consent or ask us to delete your information.
  • Opt out of promotional calls, SMS, WhatsApp or emails.

To exercise these rights, write to us at the email address below.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.
