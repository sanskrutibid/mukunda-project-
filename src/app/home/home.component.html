About Mukunda Infraventures

Mukunda Infraventure & Projects Pvt. Ltd. is the land-development company of the Mukund Group (Mukundbhai Ved Pariwar), which also works in transport, logistics and steel. It is run by its own directors from its office on Bhandara Road, Nagpur.

The company develops plotted layouts around Nagpur: residential projects at Tarodi, Jamtha, Sumthana and Wagdhara, and the Karmabhoomi industrial estate on Bhandara Road.
